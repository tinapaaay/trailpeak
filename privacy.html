Privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

In short: TrailPeak keeps only what it needs to book you on a hike. Organizers see your name and mobile number only for trips you book with them. We don't sell your data, show ads or take real payments.

Who we are

TrailPeak is a prototype for finding and joining group hikes in the Philippines, built and run by an independent developer. Organizers run the trips; TrailPeak lists them and takes reservations. To reach us, open an issue on TrailPeak's GitHub page. Don't put personal details in it; we'll reply with a private way to reach us.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email and profile picture with us; we use the name and email.
  • Your profile: your name and mobile number.
  • Your bookings: the trip, date, number of joiners, pick-up point, the e-wallet name and number you typed, and whether you chose to be listed in "Who's going".
  • The people you book for: each person's full name and whether they're 18 or older, and their mobile number if you add it. Organizers need every hiker's name to register the group at the jump-off.
  • Your emergency contact: the name, relationship and mobile number of someone not on the hike, so the organizer can reach them if something happens on the trail. Please tell them you've listed them.
  • Your reviews: the stars and text you post after a trip.
  • If you're an organizer: your organizer name, payout e-wallet and number, and whether you added business details. ID photos you choose during sign-up stay on your device; they are not uploaded.

What we don't collect

  • Card or bank numbers, or passwords. Payments are simulated: no money moves through TrailPeak yet.
  • Your location, contacts or browsing on other sites.
  • Advertising or analytics trackers. The site saves your sign-in and a copy of trip data in your browser's storage so pages load fast; that's all.

Who sees your information

  • Organizers see your name, mobile number, pick-up point, group size and DP for trips you book with them, plus the names, ages and numbers of the people you book for and your emergency contact, so they can register the group, run the trip and reach someone if needed.
  • Other joiners see only your first name and last initial (like "Ana R."), and only if you chose to be listed and they are booked on the same date. Everyone else sees only how many people are going.
  • Everyone can read your reviews, shown with your first name and last initial.
  • Services that run the site: Supabase stores accounts and bookings (in Singapore) and sends sign-in emails; Google handles "Continue with Google"; GitHub Pages hosts the site; Google Fonts and Unsplash serve fonts and photos. These services see technical details like your IP address when your browser contacts them.

We never sell your information or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

The names of people you booked for and your emergency contact are deleted 30 days after the trip. We keep your account, bookings and reviews until you ask us to delete them. Organizers may keep their own copy of joiner lists they downloaded for a trip.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173), you can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how it's used. To do any of these, open an issue on TrailPeak's GitHub page. Don't put personal details in it; we'll reply with a private way to reach us. If you think we've mishandled your data, you can also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For bigger changes, we'll tell you when you next sign in.
