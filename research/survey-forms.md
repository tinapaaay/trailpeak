# TrailPeak Google Forms (joiners and organizers)

Issue: #26 (TP-021). Two forms built from `interview-guide.md`. Copy each question into Google Forms with the **type** shown. Keep the wording: it asks about **their last real trip**, not opinions about TrailPeak.

**Plan:** joiners fill in the form only (about 5 minutes). Organizers fill in their form (about 8 minutes), then a **15-minute call** for the story questions at the end of this file.

**Google Forms settings for both:**
- Settings → Responses → **Collect email addresses: Do not collect** (we don't need them; the last question asks for a contact only if they agree to follow up).
- Settings → **Limit to 1 response: off** (it needs a Google sign-in, which puts people off).
- Settings → Presentation → **Show progress bar: on**.
- Add the privacy line from each form's intro as the form **description**.

## Form 1: Joiners (about 5 minutes)

**Title:** How do you book joiner hikes? (5 minutes)

**Description:**
> Hi! I'm building a simpler way to find and book joiner hikes in the Philippines. These questions are about your **last** hike, so there are no wrong answers. It takes about 5 minutes. Answers are anonymous unless you leave a contact at the end, and they're only used to improve the site.

### Section 1: Your last hike

1. **When was your last joiner hike?**
   Multiple choice: This month · 1–3 months ago · 4–12 months ago · More than a year ago
2. **Where did you first look for it?** *(check all that apply)*
   Checkboxes: Facebook group · Organizer's Facebook page · Instagram · TikTok · A friend sent it · Messenger group chat · Other…
3. **What did you compare before choosing an organizer?** *(check all that apply)*
   Checkboxes: Price / event fee · Date · Pick-up point · Reviews or photos from past trips · Difficulty · What's included · How fast they replied · I didn't compare, I just booked · Other…
4. **About how many messages did booking take, from your first "Hm po?" to confirmed?**
   Multiple choice: 1–5 · 6–15 · 16–30 · More than 30
5. **Was anything unclear about the fee or what's included?**
   Multiple choice: No, it was all clear · A little · Yes, I had to ask a lot
   *If "A little" or "Yes":* **What was unclear?** Short answer

### Section 2: Paying the DP

6. **How did you pay the DP?**
   Multiple choice: GCash · Maya · Bank transfer · Cash · There was no DP · Other…
7. **Before paying, how worried were you about losing the DP or being scammed?**
   Linear scale 1–5: 1 = Not worried · 5 = Very worried
8. **What made you trust that organizer?** Short answer

### Section 3: Your group

9. **Who did you book for?**
   Multiple choice: Just me · Me and 1–2 others · Me and 3 or more · Someone else booked for me
10. **Did anyone in your group drop out or send their name late?**
    Multiple choice: No · Yes, someone dropped out · Yes, names came late · Both
11. **Were you asked for an emergency contact?**
    Multiple choice: Yes, and that felt fine · Yes, and it felt like too much · No
12. **Have you ever cancelled, or had a trip cancelled on you?** Multiple choice: No · I cancelled · The organizer cancelled · Both
    *If not "No":* **What happened to your DP?** Short answer

### Section 4: What would help

13. **Would a map of trails or nearby pick-up points change which trip you'd pick?**
    Multiple choice: Yes, a lot · Maybe · Not really
14. **What information do you wish every trip post had?** Paragraph
15. **What's the one thing that would make booking a hike easier?** Paragraph
16. **Can I message you with 2–3 follow-up questions?** *(optional)*
    Short answer: "Leave a Messenger name, mobile or email, or leave blank."

## Form 2: Organizers (about 8 minutes)

**Title:** How do you run joiner trips today? (8 minutes)

**Description:**
> Hi! I'm building a tool to make running joiner hikes less work for organizers. These questions are about your **last** trip, so there are no wrong answers. It takes about 8 minutes, and then I'd love a short 15-minute call. Answers stay private and are only used to improve the tool.

### Section 1: Your last trip

1. **How many joiner trips do you run in a typical month?**
   Multiple choice: 1 · 2–3 · 4–6 · 7 or more
2. **For your last trip, how many joiners booked in the end?** Short answer (number)
3. **Where did the bookings come from?** *(check all that apply)*
   Checkboxes: Messenger · Post comments · Text/SMS · Friends of friends · Instagram · TikTok · Other…
4. **About how many messages did one booking take, from "Hm po?" to confirmed?**
   Multiple choice: 1–5 · 6–15 · 16–30 · More than 30
5. **On a busy booking day, about how many minutes do you spend on bookings (replying, checking DPs, updating the list)?** ⭐
   Short answer (number of minutes)
   *Help text:* "Your best guess is fine."

### Section 2: DP and slots

6. **When someone says "G ako" but hasn't paid the DP, how long do you wait before giving the slot to someone else?**
   Multiple choice: Less than 2 hours · 2–6 hours · 6–24 hours · More than a day · I don't give it away
7. **How do joiners pay the DP?** *(check all that apply)*
   Checkboxes: GCash · Maya · Bank transfer · Cash · Other…
8. **How do you check a DP arrived?** Short answer
9. **Has anyone paid after you'd already given the slot away?** Multiple choice: Never · Once or twice · Often
10. **Have you ever overbooked a date, or had two people pay for the last slot?** Multiple choice: Never · Once or twice · Often

### Section 3: Lists and the day itself

11. **Where do you keep the joiner list?** *(check all that apply)*
    Checkboxes: Notes app · Spreadsheet · Group chat · Paper · Other…
12. **What do you collect from each joiner?** *(check all that apply)*
    Checkboxes: Full name · Age · Mobile number · Emergency contact · Medical notes · Sex · Home city · ID number · Other…
13. **Do you register names at the jump-off or with the town/DENR?** Multiple choice: Yes, always · Sometimes · No
14. **Do you accept minors?** Multiple choice: Yes, with a parent or guardian · Yes, with a signed permission · No

### Section 4: Money and trust

15. **If a site collected the DP for you and sent it to your GCash minus a fee, how comfortable would you be?**
    Linear scale 1–5: 1 = Not comfortable · 5 = Very comfortable
16. **What fee would feel fair?** Multiple choice: Nothing, I wouldn't pay · ₱10–20 per joiner · ₱21–50 per joiner · A percent of the DP · Other…
17. **If Messenger bookings still came in, would you keep a second list up to date on a booking site?**
    Multiple choice: Yes · Only if it's quick · No
18. **What's the most annoying part of running joiner trips right now?** Paragraph
19. **Can we do a 15-minute call?** Short answer: "Messenger name, mobile or email, and good days/times."

⭐ Question 5 is the **baseline** for the pilot decision (#29): it needs at least 30% less admin time per booking. Ask it the same way after the pilot.

## Organizer call (15 minutes): stories a form can't get

Open with: "Thanks for the form! Just three stories, about your last trips." Ask "what happened next?" after each answer.

1. **Last cancellation:** "Tell me about the last time a joiner cancelled. When, why, and what happened to their DP?"
2. **Your cancelled date:** "The last time *you* cancelled a date (weather, closure, too few joiners): how did you tell people? Refund or move?"
3. **Late payment or overbooking:** pick whichever they said "Once" or "Often" to in the form: "Walk me through what happened."

If there's time: "Who decides a refund, and how fast does it go out?"
