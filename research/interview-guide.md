# TrailPeak interview guide (for later)

Issue: #26 (TP-021). Run these after the pilot build is ready. **Prefer forms?** See [survey-forms.md](survey-forms.md): the same questions as Google Forms, plus a 15-minute organizer call for the story questions. Aim for 3–5 organizers and 5 joiners, about 20–30 minutes each, by video call or in person.

**Rule for every interview:** ask about their **last real trip**, not opinions about TrailPeak. "Walk me through…" beats "Would you use…?". Write down what they *did*; keep opinions in a separate column.

Before you start: say why you're asking, that there are no wrong answers, and ask permission to take notes. Don't record names in shared notes; use "Organizer A", "Joiner 2".

---

## Organizers

### Their last departure
1. Walk me through your last trip, from the first post to the van leaving. What did you do each day?
2. Where did that post go (which groups, your page)? How many people messaged you?
3. How many joiners booked in the end? Through which channels: Messenger, comments, text, friends of friends?
4. Roughly how many messages did one booking take, from "Hm po?" to confirmed? How many minutes a day does booking take you?

### DP and holding slots (decides the 6-hour rule in #13)
5. When someone says "G ako" but hasn't paid the DP, how long do you wait before giving the slot to someone else?
6. How do they pay the DP: GCash, Maya, bank? How do you check it arrived? How long do you usually take to confirm?
7. Has anyone ever paid after you'd already given the slot away? What did you do?
8. Have you ever overbooked, or had two people pay for the last slot? What happened?

### Cancellations and refunds
9. Tell me about the last time a joiner cancelled. When, why, and what happened to their DP?
10. Tell me about the last time *you* cancelled a date (weather, closure, too few joiners). How did you tell people? Did you refund or move them?
11. Who decides a refund, and how fast does it go out?

### Lists, safety and the day itself
12. How do you keep the joiner list today (notes, spreadsheet, chat)? What do you need from each joiner: full name, age, emergency contact?
13. Do you register names at the jump-off? What happens when a joiner sends names late?
14. How do you handle minors?

### Trust and money
15. If a site collected the DP for you and sent it to your GCash minus a fee, what would worry you?
16. What fee would feel fair, per joiner or as a percent? What would make it worth paying?
17. Would you keep a second booking list up to date if Messenger bookings still came in? What would make that easy?

### Close
18. What's the most annoying part of running joiner trips right now?
19. Can I follow up after a few trips with TrailPeak?

---

## Joiners

### Their last trip
1. Walk me through how you found and booked your last hike. Where did you look first?
2. How did you choose between organizers? What did you compare (price, date, pick-up, reviews, difficulty)?
3. How many messages did booking take? Was anything unclear about the fee or what's included?
4. How did you pay the DP? Did you worry about losing it or being scammed? What made you trust that organizer?

### Groups and details
5. Did you book alone or for a barkada? Who sent the names? Did anyone drop out?
6. Were you asked for an emergency contact? How did that feel?

### Cancellations
7. Have you ever cancelled or had a trip cancelled? What happened to your DP?

### Discovery (decides what to build after the pilot)
8. Would a map of trails or nearby pick-up points have changed which trip you picked? Why or why not?
9. What information do you wish every trip post had?

### Close
10. What's the one thing that would make booking a hike easier?

---

## After each interview
- Fill in: channels used, minutes/messages per booking, DP hold time, refund story, trust worries, fee reaction.
- Note anything that contradicts the PRD's assumptions (6-hour hold, commission, maps).
- Update the PRD's open decisions and issue #13's hold numbers if the answers point somewhere else.
