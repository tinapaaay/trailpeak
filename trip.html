        How booking works

        1. Reserve. Pick a date and how many are joining. Your slots are held for a few hours (shorter when the trip is soon); the exact deadline shows as soon as you reserve.
        2. Send the DP to the organizer. Pay them directly by GCash or Maya, then enter the reference number in My trips. That keeps your slots while they check it. With no reference by the deadline, the slots are released.
        3. The organizer confirms. Your booking then shows "DP received". TrailPeak doesn't handle or check the money; you pay the balance to the organizer on meetup day.
        4. Rain or a closure. If PAGASA raises signal no. 1 or higher or the LGU closes the trail, you can move to another date or get your DP back.
        5. Cancelling. 7 or more days before, move to another date once, free. Later than that, or a no-show, the DP isn't refunded.
        6. Questions. Tap Ask the organizer for their mobile number. After you reserve, it's also in My trips.

        Cancellation policy

        • Bad weather or trail closure (PAGASA signal no. 1 or higher, or an LGU closure): move to another date or get your DP back.
        • Organizer cancels: full DP refund within 3 days.
        • You cancel 7+ days before: move to another date once, free.
        • You cancel less than 7 days before, or don't show up: the DP is not refunded.
