Terms of service

Last updated November 10, 2026

In short: TrailPeak helps you find and reserve group hikes. Organizers run the trips and are responsible for them. TrailPeak is a prototype: payments are simulated and features may change. Hike safely and follow your guides.

What TrailPeak is

TrailPeak lists joiner hikes and group climbs posted by independent organizers and lets you reserve a slot. TrailPeak doesn't run trips, employ guides or provide transport. The organizer named on each trip does.

TrailPeak is a prototype. Payments are simulated, so no money moves through it, and ID and business checks are not real yet. Features can change or stop without notice.

Your account

  • Use your real name and a mobile number that reaches you. Organizers rely on them on meetup day.
  • Keep access to your email or Google account safe; anyone with it can sign in as you.
  • Organizers must be 18 or older. Joiners under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission, and the organizer's.

Joining a trip

  • Read the trip page before you reserve: difficulty, what the fee covers, pick-up times and the cancellation policy.
  • Each trip's cancellation policy decides refunds and date changes. Under the standard policy, you can move a booking once, free, 7 or more days before; a late cancellation or a no-show keeps the DP.
  • Choose trips that match your fitness and experience, follow the organizer's and guides' instructions, and respect local rules, communities and Leave No Trace.

Posting trips (organizers)

  • Post trips you actually run, with accurate dates, fees, inclusions, pick-up points and slots.
  • Get the permits, guides and registrations each mountain requires, and follow DENR and local government rules, including closures.
  • Keep to your cancellation policy. If you cancel a date (for weather or anything else), tell your joiners and offer what the policy promises.
  • Use joiners' names and numbers only to run the trips they booked.
  • Use only photos you took or have permission to use.

Reviews

Only joiners who booked a trip can review it, once, after the trip date. Keep reviews honest and about the trip. We may remove reviews that are abusive, false, or share someone's private details.

Hiking has risks

Trails can be steep, slippery and remote, and weather changes fast. You join at your own risk. To the extent Philippine law allows, TrailPeak isn't responsible for injuries, losses or costs from a trip, or for what organizers or other joiners do. Organizers are responsible for the trips they run.

Ending or changing accounts

You can stop using TrailPeak any time and ask us to delete your account. We may suspend accounts that break these terms, post false trips, or misuse other people's information.

The law that applies

These terms follow the laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

Contact and changes

For questions, open an issue on TrailPeak's GitHub page. Don't put personal details in it; we'll reply with a private way to reach us. If we change these terms, we'll update the date at the top. See also the Privacy policy.
